Inventaire

Walk the fridge and pantry. Tap anything you're out of — it turns blue. The blue ones become your shopping list.

Or skip checking off: scan your receipt and it records the trip for you.

Receipt reading

Inventaire reads receipts and recipes with Claude, using your own Anthropic API key. Each scan costs a few cents on your API account, and the photo is sent to Anthropic to be read.

The key stays on this phone. It isn't included in backups.

Get a key at platform.claude.com under Settings → API keys.
